Transparent & Explainable Risk Engine

Mathematical rule-based scoring engine with fully transparent feature weighting and interactive threshold calibration.

Deterministic Mathematical Model
MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

Composite Risk Scoring Model: $R = \sum_{i} (w_i \times \Delta_i)$

AeroReady AI intentionally avoids "black-box" machine learning predictions for flight-critical governance. Instead, it computes an objective, normalized composite risk score ($0 - 100$) derived from 7 telemetry vectors relative to engineering limits.

Exhaust Gas Temp (EGT) Weight: 25% $\Delta > 780^\circ\text{C}$
Bearing Vibration Weight: 20% $\Delta > 0.45\text{ IPS}$
Oil Pressure Delta Weight: 15% $\Delta < 42\text{ PSI}$
Flight Hour Load Weight: 10% $Hours / 1500$
Component Age Weight: 10% $Years / 10$
Inspection Interval Weight: 10% $\Delta > 45\text{ days}$
Fault Log History Weight: 10% $Count \times 2.5$

Fleet Availability & What-If Scenario Simulator

Quantitative simulation modeling the impact of proactive risk scheduling vs reactive failure downtime on overall air power availability.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULA
$$\text{Fleet Availability } A = \frac{N_{\text{serviceable}}}{N_{\text{total}}} \times 100\% = \frac{N_{\text{total}} - (N_{\text{scheduled}} + N_{\text{unscheduled\_failures}})}{N_{\text{total}}} \times 100\%$$

Trade-off Principle: Proactive servicing incurs predictable planned minor downtime (8–14 hrs) but prevents catastrophic component seizure that causes uncontained failure (28–60 hrs downtime + emergency part backorders).
